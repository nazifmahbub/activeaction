The Job Ready Pro Program

In today's competitive job market, having the right credentials isn't enough. Your resume needs to pass AI screening tools, your LinkedIn profile needs to attract recruiters, and your overall professional brand needs to tell a compelling story.

The Job Ready Pro Program is a focused, 4-week 1:1 intensive designed to give you a complete career toolkit. Whether you're a newcomer to Canada, a career changer, or a seasoned professional looking to level up, this program will transform how you present yourself to employers.

Who Is This For?

Internationally trained professionals and newcomers to Canada
Recent graduates entering the job market for the first time
Mid-career professionals looking to transition industries
Anyone who has been sending applications without results
Professionals who know they're underselling themselves

What You'll Walk Away With

ATS-optimized resume — crafted to pass AI filters and impress human recruiters
LinkedIn profile overhaul — keyword-rich, professionally branded, recruiter-magnet profile
Custom cover letter framework — adaptable templates that tell your story powerfully
Professional portfolio — showcase your work, achievements, and capabilities
Interview preparation — STAR method coaching and mock interview practice
Job search strategy — a targeted action plan for finding and applying to the right roles
Networking scripts — how to reach out to recruiters and connections effectively

Program Format

This is a 4-week, 1:1 intensive delivered virtually. Each week focuses on a specific pillar of your professional brand. Between sessions, you'll complete targeted assignments, and Dr. Nazif will provide detailed written feedback on your materials.

1
Week 1: Discovery, positioning, and resume audit & rebuild
2
Week 2: LinkedIn optimization and personal brand development
3
Week 3: Cover letters, portfolio, and application strategy
4
Week 4: Interview coaching, networking, and job search launch
