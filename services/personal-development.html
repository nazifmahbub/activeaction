Personal Development Coaching

Build the mindset, habits, and skills to create lasting change — in just 4 weeks.

Home/ Services/ Personal Development

What Is This Program?

The Personal Development Coaching program is a 4-week intensive 1:1 experience designed for driven individuals who want to break through barriers, build powerful habits, and step into the best version of themselves.

Dr. Nazif combines evidence-based coaching frameworks with his background in public health and behavioral science to deliver a program that's not just motivating — it's transformational. You'll walk away with clarity on your goals, a concrete action plan, and the tools to sustain your growth long after the program ends.

Who Is This For?

This program is ideal for:

Professionals feeling stuck or unfulfilled and ready for meaningful change
Newcomers to Canada navigating career transitions and cultural adaptation
Ambitious individuals who want to upgrade their mindset and daily habits
Leaders seeking to grow their influence and emotional intelligence
Anyone wanting accountability, clarity, and a personalized growth roadmap

What's Included

4 weekly 1:1 coaching sessions (60 minutes each) with Dr. Nazif
Guided self-assessment to identify your core strengths, gaps, and goals
Personalized action plan tailored to your specific situation
Practical tools, worksheets, and habit-tracking frameworks
Access to the Transform Yourself course (complementary)
Email support between sessions for questions and accountability
Post-program resources and strategy guide for continued growth

The Approach

Dr. Nazif's coaching is rooted in behavioral science, positive psychology, and lived experience. He uses a structured yet flexible framework that adapts to your unique circumstances. Sessions are conducted virtually (Zoom/Google Meet) and are available to clients anywhere in the world.

This is not a one-size-fits-all program. Every session, every exercise, every recommendation is tailored to you — your goals, your story, your timeline.

"By day I work in public service shaping policies for Alberta's health and wellbeing. By evenings and weekends I coach clients 1:1, because I genuinely believe that every person has untapped potential waiting to be activated."

— Dr. Nazif Mahbub
