Coaching from Someone Who's Been There

Applying to an MPH program in Canada as an internationally trained health professional is one of the most challenging and opaque processes you'll encounter. The requirements are complex, the competition is fierce, and the guidance available is often generic and unhelpful.

Dr. Nazif Mahbub did it himself. As an internationally trained physician from Bangladesh, he successfully gained admission to the MPH program at the University of Alberta. He knows what Canadian admissions committees look for, how to position your international experience powerfully, and how to navigate the unique challenges that internationally trained professionals face.

This coaching service is his way of giving back — helping others avoid the mistakes he made and accelerate the journey he navigated alone.

Who Is This For?

Internationally trained physicians, nurses, and health professionals in Canada
Newcomers to Canada wanting to transition into public health
Health professionals seeking career advancement through graduate education
Anyone who has been rejected from MPH programs and wants to strengthen their application

What's Included

Program selection guidance — Identifying the best MPH programs in Canada for your background and goals
Application strategy — How to position your international experience competitively
Statement of purpose coaching — Writing a compelling, tailored personal statement
CV/resume review — Formatting your academic and professional experience for Canadian standards
Reference letter strategy — How to approach referees and what to request
Interview preparation — Mock admissions interviews with detailed feedback
Timeline planning — Application calendars and deadline management
Insider knowledge — What Canadian admissions committees actually look for

Key Canadian MPH Programs

Dr. Nazif provides guidance for applications to programs at leading Canadian institutions including:

University of Alberta (Dr. Nazif's own alma mater)
University of Toronto (Dalla Lana School of Public Health)
University of British Columbia
McGill University
Simon Fraser University & other major programs
