About Dr. Nazif

A Leader at the Intersection of Health, Policy & Human Development

Dr. Nazif Mahbub is a physician, public health leader, certified health executive, and Fellow of the Royal Society for Public Health (UK). By day, he shapes health policy as a Senior Policy Analyst with the Government of Alberta. By evenings and weekends, he coaches clients one-on-one, records award-winning podcast episodes, develops courses, and builds free resources for his global community.

His journey spans two continents — from earning his MBBS at the University of Dhaka to completing an MPH in Health Policy & Management at the University of Alberta. He co-founded Active Action Organization, a non-profit dedicated to helping newcomers integrate into Canada, and launched Active Action Lab to serve professionals worldwide.
