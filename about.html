The Story

From Dhaka to Edmonton — A Journey of Purpose

Dr. Nazif Mahbub's story is one of grit, ambition, and an unwavering commitment to making a difference. Born in Bangladesh, he trained as a physician at the University of Dhaka, where he earned his Bachelor of Medicine and Surgery (MBBS). Driven by a passion for systemic change, he immigrated to Canada and went on to earn a Master of Public Health (MPH) in Health Policy & Management from the University of Alberta.

Today, Dr. Nazif serves as a Senior Policy Analyst in Primary Health Profession Regulation and Preventative Health Services with the Government of Alberta — shaping policies that affect the health and wellbeing of Albertans every single day.

But his impact doesn't stop at the office door. Outside of his government role, he is the founder of Active Action Lab, a personal and professional development platform with 45,000+ community members across 15+ countries. He coaches professionals 1:1, delivers corporate workshops, produces an award-winning podcast, and has built one of the most engaged immigrant professional communities in Canada.

He co-founded Active Action Organization, a non-profit dedicated to helping newcomers successfully integrate into Canada through health promotion, social awareness, and community support.
